You don't need to be so tensed, Try to be relaxed like us: pinguins, flowers, green fields, sea, clean streets, smiling people, huge parks, pubs and clubs. You see, we can't be but relaxed in our country. By the way, how is your country ?

We are now in Christchurch, the biggest city in the south island. Still, there is no sense of a big city - all people are living in their private houses with a nice backyard. The city center is composed of a big circus with a beautiful church. There is also a huge park with big (free) botanic gardens which we'll see tomorrow. You see it doesn't feel like big city - no traffics, beeping or pollution or dirt on the streets.

So what did we do until now (it's our third day here) - we bought a car (picture of a car like ours attached)

It's a fansy minivan, Mitzubishi Chariot Mx, 1987, automatic second hand. Including: electric windows, erlectric mirrors adjastment, power stirring wheel, stereo cassette, central lock, alarm. It just passed WOF (like test), and it hardly have a scratch. As you can see from the pictures it's not the best looking car, so at the auction we were the only ones who wanted it (we did our homeworks before and checked the cars before the auction). The price is almost 3500 shekels.

We are staying with a nice family, it's for free - it's part of the hit (hosting Israeli travellers). We don't know why, but they love israelis, and they are very nice, always trying to help, we feel uneasy with that and try to avoid eating dinner on their expense, They are not that rich.

Now, with the car, starts our real journey of exploring New-Zealand. We take our time, though, and we'll be in this city for 2 days more.
