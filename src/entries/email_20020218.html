Hello to all of you !!

We hope you're OK. Every day there are news from Israel, and most of it is not good. We worry about you guys !!!. Watch out !!!.

We also watch out from our car window and see spectacular views: green fields, bays that go deep into the land, lots of ship, some deers, bunnies, horses, hawks, gooses, ducks, oposums, seagulls, huge pegeons etc.

We've been to a place next to Christchurch (70 km away), Akaroa. It's was created from volcanic erruption long time ago, it has alot of bays or harbours. Tourists go there mainly to swim with dolphins. We saved most of this money and took a kayak and saw 3 of them passing us by. The ones here are very small (1-1.5 m) and has a special blue-grey colour.

Most of the people do here the banks paninsula trek. We saved here and managed our own car trek, and it was worth a while cause we visited all of the bays (7-8 of them), and they are magnificant.

Before that we went to the zoo that specialized in kiwis (the bird not the people). They put the birds in a special room where they dim the light so the kiwis will think it's night time and will be active (they sleep in the day time). We saw them looking for food, vacuuming the earth using there funny nose/pick. They walk very funny, we even touched them and they were not afraid - they are really cute.

We're planning on going to Kaikura to see whales, and that as north as we'll get for now. after that we'll go to Hanmer hot-springs - returning to our old and favourite habit - sitting in the hotsprings and doind nothing. After that we'll continue on the beautiful road on our way to Abel-pass and then south.

Greeting you to have success in whatever you do (or not do)
