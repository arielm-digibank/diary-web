Hi Guys !!!

Tonight we go to Ko-samoi, in 5-6 days we'll continue to Ko-pangan and there we'll be there for the new-year. I guess it'll be fun.

Already in Bangkok life is fun, but we got tired of this, and we'll explain:

1. We got tired of having oil massage for 20 shekel, that last an hour, and make you feel like you're flowting.

2. We're tired of the fruit-shakes, made up of real fruits, for 2 shekels.

3. We are tired of the barbique we have everywhere on the street, one "Shipud" for 1 shekel.

4. We are tired of local women start to talk to us thinking we're cheap.

5. We are tired of seeing women walking in very short cloths, frequently braless.

6. We are tired of seeing happy people walking on the street with a stupid smile on their face.

7. We are tired from all these cheap cocktails (5 shekel) we can get anywhere here.

8. We are tired of walking in te streets at 5 am and seeing that alot of places are still open and many people are still having fun there.

9. We are tired from the cheap internet (3 shekel an hour), cheap phone (1 shekel a minute), and seeing channel 1&2 from Israel. Yesterday we watche Maccabi game, our heart isn't used to such stresses, (ofcourse we didn't see the news).

10. We are tired of seeing sex-shows and strippers and paying only for a drink.

That's why we leave this place !!!!

Have fun you all !!!
