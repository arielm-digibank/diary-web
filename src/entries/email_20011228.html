Ko-[pangan, just before the full-moon & new-year parties
Fri, 28 Dec 2001 09:05:14 -0800 (PST)
Hello everyone !!!

We decided to write, cause some people got alarmed that maybe we forgot them cause we're having fun. Yes, we're having fun, but we didn't forget no one. The only problem is always the price. So we'll be more specific and personal when we'll get back to bangkok (should be 3/1/2002).

We are now in Ko-pangan, preparing for the parties. The full moon is tomorrow. All the parties are on the beach. Some people who put some money go to the waterfalls for hard-core trance + parties. We are saving, so don't be alarmed. The island and especially this area of the island (called hadreen) is full of people, especially israelis. I squeezed myself in some israelis place here. Gonen went to the near island, Ko-samoi and he's taking the ferry here for the parties.

In between we've been 3 days in Ko-tao, an island in this area which specialized in diving places. We found it the most beautiful of all the 3 islands, and some of the beaches there are real classics. We took shnorkels and watched the under-water life.

That's it for now, more will be in a week.

Don't forget to celebrate new-year !!!!
