Back to Thailand, summary of Laos
Sat, 26 Jan 2002 04:11:25 -0800 (PST)
Hello guys !!!

It's been a while from our last Email. We were 2 weeks in places without electricity.

We are now in Chang-Mai, North Thailand. This is a big and cheap city. We also managed to finish a 3 day trek in the area (we call it a nice walking trip). It includes Bamboo rafting (a bambook raphsody) and elephant ride. Gonen advanced to the elephant driver seat (on his neck). We chose the biggest, the only male elephant and we got ourselves an easy ride. No stopping to eat, no blowing dust on the travelers. The descent service you usually get from a male.

Tomorrow we'll go to pie. Gonen wants to hire a big motorbike here and drive the road ourselves (140 km on the road, 200 km on the country side roads). We'll look for a good offer or we'll go there by bus and travel from there.

A word about Laos. It's amazing. This place is really worth visit.

We told you that Vang-Vieng is a great place, so we found another one - Mong-Ngoy. It's a very small town, the view from the guest house is awsome, and the prices is the cheapest (4 shekel for a double room). We drank local whisky (Lao-Lao) with some locals, and went to a local "hanukat bait". Tourist go there for the peace and quiet this place gives. When Gonen start to fire bombers (napatzim) at them, it wasn't quiet any more. The local children loved it and demanded more.

In the biggest cities we only stopped for a night if we didn't have a choise.

Another cute place is mount-Sing, near the chinese border. We went to the border and no one stopped us so we crossed it and got some pictures near chinese signs and returned. We went to some local authentic villiges and were disappointed to see they were not authentic anymore. We saw some topless women, but everybody, especially the small kids wanted us to buy things or to give them whatever we had (we were lucky we only had some sweets which were taken from us rudely by the kids). In the evening Gonen went to a temple and somehow managed to drink some lao-lao also there. Then he joined me in a pub (the only one who is open after 21:00), exploded some bombers and lao people were really terified, the echo was very good, making it sounds pretty loud. Gonen also broke a chair (by sitting on it, and he weigh less than before).

Peace and quiet were restored only when we left.

We got stuck before the border. The pick-up (a jeep) broke down.

Laos is very beautiful and very cheap (250$ a month, less than India). You should visit there if you plan to go to Thailand.

There is more ofcourse, but the Email got too long

We'll write again before we'll fly to New-Zealand

Take care we want to see you some day

Sir Gonen & Sir Malik
