Hi Everyone !!!

We're back in Asia, in the Philippines. We're back to the big mess of the far east. At the beginning we weren't prepared for that, but after that we started to enjoy. Now we're sorry we have only 3 weeks here, there are so many things to see.

It's quite hot here, around 35 celcius and quite humid too.

Manila is a big mess. A lot of traffic, there are colorful jeeps (jipney) which are used as a pick-up (between taxi and a bus). There are some houses, especially churches, that are built in spanish style, different than what we've seen. The prices are cheap again, we like that, we like to eat in the street - especially chicken ("pulke") or wings. The mango here is the best we've eaten, much different than in Israel.

The experience is much different than we've seen before. One must be here to understand.

Now we're in Cebu, a different island. This is the second large city in Philippines.

Everybody call us "hey Joe", thinking we're cool Americans or refer to us as "sir" (and we like it) when they want to sell us things. Up until now the people here are among the nicest one we've met.

In the first 2 days we've been hosted in Melody's nephew house and we've been treated as kings. They fullfilled all our needs, they even didn't let us go to the toilet alone. As many times before we drank beer & Brendy with the locals until we've become drunk.

There are hardly any tourists here. Hardly any Israelis and we like it.

So for now we're having fun, hope you have too

Sir Joe Gonen
