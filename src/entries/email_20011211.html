Hi Guys !!

We are now in Bangkok, Thailand.

This place is totally different than any other place we've been.

First of all it's very modern, at least like Israel. A lot of roads with new cars, lots of new and modern buildings and molls. Yet there is a lot of signs of the past. Big "Pagodot", still they use the canals with boats serve as buses.

We assume the rate of the old and the new is 20:80 (80 percent new).

A lot of tourists here from all over the world, still the presence of the Israeli tourists is noticable, a lot of signs in hebrew.

The girls here are pretty. The local and the tourists. The heat adds to that cause they wear very short cloths (and very often forget to wear a bra).

But the difference between local women and local men is very small, one can easely mistake a man as a woman (these are called "lady boy").

We are affraid to say  to a woman "boy lyady" and after to find out it's a "lady boy".

The food here is great, especially different fruits and meat. They have great shakes. They also fry any kind of meat they find. They sell it in the street for 1 Shekel for "Shipud" (fruit shake costs 2 shekel)

Today we went to see the city, and to our surprise we saw there are many things to see and to do. A lot of "Pagodot" and very beautiful buildings.

Tonight we intend to get an oil message for 20 shekels an hour.

Yesterday we were confused when we arrived. Today we came to our senses. We decided to we love the city. We will stay here until the end of the week or until the beginning of next week.

Thats it for now

Wev'e been in Beit Habad in Katmandu for "Hanuka" so we don't miss Israel.

Take care back home, it's dangerous out there !!!
