Fiordland, one of the most beautiful places on earth
Tue, 5 Mar 2002 13:39:23 -0800 (PST)
Hi guys (and some girls) !!!

We didn't write for a long time, internet is a little bit expensive away from big cities.

We're back on trek (we're trekking). (We hope you keep on track with our trekking).

We've been to the south of the south island (called Catlings). We saw some wildlife there and our list of animals to see is almost finished.

If you remember the story about Gonen & the seal from last time, well, the story repeated itself big time - with sea lion which is double size of the seal and of Gonen (almost 3 meter long and about 500 kg). Gonen wanted to take a good picture of the sea-lion and Malik standing behind it. When Gonen got too close the sea lion roared and showed his teeth it was scary. When he raised it upper body he was Gonen's height. Did he get a good picture ? We'll know later cause Gonen pressed on the button and ran away at the same time.

We almost ran over a cute, yellow-eyed pinguin which decided to cross the road in front of us, this was at nugget point, a very beautiful place, a view point with some wild cliff's and down you can see sea-lion playing and enjoying the sun.

In porpoise beach we saw some Dolphins jumping out of the water and playing. When Malik said he'll enter the cold water to swim with them Gonen was sure he'll get cold feet. Malik entered. The dolphins slowly swam away. People, sitting on the beach wearing their sweatshirts got uppset cause Malik scared the dolphins away, when Malik got out he got cold feet (also his rest of the body was cold).

After spending 2-3 days in the Catlings, and the weekend in Invercargil, we went to the fiordland, Milford sound. It was the first time the weather dissapointed us and it was raining hard all the time. The sky were grey. Still we managed to see the great potential of this place, that's why we're going back there now.

On our way there we saw a big bird, hawk-like, but it didn't get scared and didn't fly a way when we stopped near, so we fed her with some carrot. It sat on one of our open window, almost got into our car. It sat on the roof waiting to be fed. Later we discovered it's the Kea, a very bold bird that gets closer to people and grab their stuff. We, Israelis, like this "hutzpa".

We met so many nice people from the HIT (Hosting Israeli Tourists) program. They host us in their homes for free (or almost for free). They are warm people and their hospitality is great. They like Israeils, still we don't know why, but we try not to spoile this impression. Maybe New-Zealand is the only place on earth where Israelis are warm-welcomed.

We can continue and tell more stories but enough.

So we hope we'll write more soon.

For our Israeli people - be carefull when you drive, when you go to the mall, to restaurants, going on a bus, being in a central bus station, those who are still in the army, those who do reserves, living in the occupied territories, walking in the street and seeing an arab-like man passing you by, ...... - we worry about you. Can't you live in peace without us ???

We need to worry not to ran over bunnies that keep on jumping in front of our car.

Cool Gonen

Chilled Malik
