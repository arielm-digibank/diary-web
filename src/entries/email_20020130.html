Pictures from trekking in north Thailand
Wed, 30 Jan 2002 23:24:32 -0800 (PST)
Hi all of you !!!

We got some pictures from a trek we've done in north Thailand about a week ago. The pictures were taken by an Israeli guy from the group.

We returned from Pai yesterday. Pai is a great town, lies in a beautiful valley. We took a great (a bit costy - 20 shekel) place on the bank of the river with a great view. We didn't buy beer in the evening so we could afford it.

Pai has a very nice hot spring. Open 24 hours, free. We went there twice a day. We found that hot springs are a sheer pleasure, to the body and to the soul. We consider making this a habbit. We added candles in the night and music and made it a perfect experience.

We took a big motorbike, Kawasaki and Gonen drove it for 500km in 72 hours. This was worth a while cause the scenary is great, and it best viewed that way, as part of the landscape. The road was all turns and curves, so the driving was very tiring. Gonen wants a rest from motorbikes for a while.

But the highlight of this trip, and by now the best nature phenomena we've seen in our 4 month of travelling was Lod cave, 50 km north west to Pai. We drove there one day, just cause we didn't have anything left to see in Pai and we didn't want to lie around doing nothing

The cave considered to be the second largest cave (by volume) in the south east Asia. It's 600m long, 20m wide and 50m high. There is a river going from one end of the cave to the other. We did it with a bamboo raphsody. There are fish in the river and you can feed them in the middle of the cave, and they come houndrads fighting for the food. We also walked inside it, and the stagelites and other shapes of rocks created by nature were fascinating, big, and unique. What we saw in all the caves in Laos and more was in this cave. But the most amazing thing happened at sunset. Exactly at 6Pm the birds came, out of nowhere, circled outside and flew into the cave with a speed of a bullet. Tens of Thousands of them. At 6:30, everything finished. If you entered the cave at that time you felt it was snowing (which was just bird sheet). Amazing stuff !!!

That's it, tonight we take a bus to Bangkok. Our flight to Australia is on the 2nd.

By the way, this morning when Gonen was sleeping I (Malik) went to do bunjy jump to a lake, 50m, costs only 25$. It was nice.

Enjoy the pictures (there is also part 2). Sorry for the bad quality.

Have fun
