New-Zealand, enchanting Dunedin
Sat, 9 Feb 2002 23:00:06 -0800 (PST)
Hello dear mates, how is it going ?

We are now in one of the most beautiful places we've seen so far. It's a small town with houses with backyards full of flowers. In the background there are green mountains. To the east there is the sea shore, exceptionally clean - even though there are no trash cans.

We are only 2 days here but we seems to understand the nature of this place - it's a student town with a big night scene - drinking and dancing and laughing. All through the night you can see drunk and happy people (not in a violent way like in Israel).

Yesterday we found ourselves, accidently, in a middle of a scotish festival - groups with beautiful scotish traditional uniform (skirts) with pipes & big drums. The road was closed and they marched in playing some scotish tunes. It was a competition and it lasted for hours.

Dunedin is not very touristic and the local residents are charming, very helpfull - way too kind in our standards.

We still eat cheaply here: 15 shekel for burgerking meal (refill drinks) or 6 shekel for fish& chips.

Yesterday we also saw the magnificant botanic gardens (free of charge ofcourse), there are big variaty of plants & flowers and also a nice river in the middle. The beauty is hard to describe. One must be there to understand it.

Somehow, the steepest street in the world is here, Baldwin street. Unfortanetly  we decided to stroll along this road - what ended up as a hard climb. At the end of the road there was a tap (how surprising) and cars are not allowed to park parallel to the sidewalk.

Today we went to the shores of Dunedin.

Tomorrow we'll take the bus to Christchurch, 5 hours drive. There we'll buy a car and start to explore the country.

You are sorry you're not here. (we also sorry for you).

REL and his mate Gonen
