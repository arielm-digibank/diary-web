Ko-Samoi - heaven
Tue, 18 Dec 2001 03:46:26 -0800 (PST)
Hello to you !!

We are here in Ko-samoi (3 days now).

The island is very beautiful, yet very developed. Reminding Rodus.

Yet, the beaches are far more beautiful. Getting away from the center of the island (chawang), you find very beautiful beaches, full of palm trees, white sands.Its fun playing "matkot" their, playing beach volley-ball, or just sit, drink shakes or read books (not us)

The weather is changing constantly, the sun can disappear and heavy rain starts and vice versa.

There is some activities: taking jet-ski, elephant ride or hiring bikes. Gonen and his girlfriend chose to take a harly-davidson-like monstrous bike and explored the island. When he reached a dream-like view point, he asked his girlfriend to pinch him to see that he's not dreaming. He told me he was afraid he'll wake up at home back in Israel and he was happy to see he's still here on the island. By the way, It costed them only 30 shekel for 24 hours.

A lot of sea food. Malik like to eat shark ("it sure beats getting eaten by a shark" he says) or squid. Gonen settles for a red snipper for 15 shekel, he also tried and enjoyed shrimps.

No need to go to sleep early. A lot of pubs, discos, sometimes there is a big party on the beach (we were in a "black-moon party"), so we go out every night.

In 3 days we'll leave this island and go to Ko-pangan, the highlight should be the full-moon and new-year parties. We will meet there a lot of friends that we met in India & Nepal, so we expect good-time there also.

We have such fun we envy ourselves. 

We think about you (at least once every few days), take care and be in touch !!!
