Caves in Laos
Fri, 11 Jan 2002 06:39:59 -0800 (PST)
Hello to you !!

We are now in Laos, Vang-vieng, a small town 3 hours drive from vang-tien, the capital.

Pretty soon this place reached the status of heaven (like norht-India and Pokhara, Nepal).

The view is wild. Mountains, but very different from Nepal or any usual mountain. They like pop up from the ground, very sharp. They are green, because they get a lot of rain (in the season, not now). Of course, this causes caves, and they are here in dozens. We visited 4 already. In one we walked for a kilometer inside (Yeah, that's true) until we get to a pool inside it. We could continue cause the tunnel continued, but the guide told us to go back. Amazing !!!.

Other caves we visited had some incredible shapes caused by nature. Our "ntifim" cave looks pretty small compare to the ones we saw here. It's probabely less touristics here, cause we use a flash-light when we go into the caves.

Today we took bicycles to explore the area, saw 2 caves and finished with a great sunset from the river bank. Our butts aches Yesterday we took tubes. The stream is very weak, so you just relax and enjoy the great scenary.

Prices are redicilous: the bikes costs 5 shekel a day, the tube: 2.5 shekel. Stake or shnitzel (with chips) costs 5-7.5 shekel, a shake costs 1-2 shekel. We don't save, we just eat more.

So, Laos is a great place. The local people are very nice and now very little English.

We plan to continue North, the places are less touristic, so we probabely will lose connection (temporarily) with the outside world.

Still enjoying our trip
