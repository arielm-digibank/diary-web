Back from the trek !!
Thu, 29 Nov 2001 02:17:09 -0800 (PST) 
Hello to you !!!
We are back from the Annaporna trek after 14 not easy
days.
 
This 200 km trek provided great views and landscapes,
we saw nature at it's best. It was warm, it was very
cold (indeed, except Malik who took cold shower at
3800 m).
We walked along one river on our way up from 600m to
5416m (Throng pass, the world widest mountain pass),
The view changed from green forest to desert and to
snow. On the other side we went down the famous
KaliGantaki river, and the view was a little bit
different.
 
We met a lot of nice local people (even though we
didn't use the services of a porter nor guide), we
carried our own stuff (10-15 kg). Gonen is called now
Ari(k) Po(r)ter.
 
We were group of 5 and then 6 and then 7 people, we
stayed together despite all the difficulties.
 
Now we're at Pokhara eating all the food we didn't
have in this Trek (or was incredebely expensive).
 
So, we're OK,
 
In the next 2-3 days We'll be going to Katmandu, only
there we will go over our mail, so be patience.
 
Bless you all
 
The tired, yet pleased,
 
Sir   Sir

Gonen Malik
