Hi All guys !!!
 
Thanks for waiting for us until now. We're travelling
through small towns and Email, if finding one, is very
expensive. Time goes by and we have so many responses
to give - so we looked for a cheap one. Now in a very
nice HIT house in the town of Gisborne, we were alowed
to use the Internet here.
 
From last time:
We had 2 seders, attached a picture from the second
one, with 80 people, mostly christians, we were 4
israelis. Gonen, Malik and another couple. They
treated us as celebrities.
 
After that we spent some days around the Egmont
mountain (Taranaki). On the 1st of April we had snow,
one of the coldest days of this season for several
years over here.
 
We went to do the Tungariro crossing trek, it was very
beautiful. It was a beautiful day but there was snow
and near the snow hot steam coming of the ground. It
was our longest trek (7 hours) and it will stay like
that. I under estimated the trek and walked with my
sandals. It was rediculous near all the european with
their advanced equipment. We started a snow war. Gonen
used his abilities as an artilary man and shoot some
pretty accurate ones from behind rocks, hitting me in
my weakest spot - The sandals.
 
After that we went to lake Taupo, the largest lake of
New-Zealand. We rested all the time (from what ???) -
went to hot springs (of course) and went out at night.
Gonen played pool and reached the semi-final. Gonen
got chears from all the Israelis there but we finished
the night paying for our own drinks (the winner gets
his drinks for free).
 
We went to the east, to Napier, a little bit of track,
most of the travellers don't go there, and found a
small and very cute town. We continued to Urewera
national park and saw beautiful lakes and waterfalls.
Now, we really got of track, nobody is here in
Gisborne and we'll see what that means.
 
That's all in brief, not many jokes this time, too
much to tell.
We follow what's going on in Israel, We're very
worried. To all the people in Israel - take care.
