"Pesach Sameach" from North Island
Mon, 25 Mar 2002 23:43:27 -0800 (PST)
Hi to you all !!!

We are now at the northern island, Wellington.

This is a big city, (the capital). We arrived here yesterday with the big ferry (including our car).

In Wellington we've been to a very nice Museum (yes it can happen), the Te Papa Museum, showing animals, Erruptions, Galleries, Maori Heritage etc.. We enjoyed it that means it's cool.

Last time we wrote we had a cloudy + rainy weather. After Gonen took out his hat the sun came out. We didn't let him put his hat back and the sun stayed.

We rented a jet boat with the help of navy graduate Dani and saw all the beautiful bays of Abel Tasman. We saw some seals and Malik swam with them.

We all took turns in driving the boat. Gonen drove fiercely and we all got our butt aches.

Day after that we took our car to another car-trek day (until now 9000km in 45 days) and saw magnificant bays on our way to the French Pass. Gonen saved an Opposum from being ran over by our car. In New-Zealand it's recommended to run over Opposum cause they kill the trees, but we're loving animals. By the way, the meat is very cheap and delicious, we tasted rabbit meat and it was fine.

Day after that we went to Nelson Lakes area and got bitten by the sand flies.

To all those who celebrate Pesach - Happy (and kosher ?) Pesach.

Enjoy your "kneidalach", we're working on ours, meaning maybe we'll get/cook/wash the dishes for them.

Happy Pesach

Shark Gonen,
