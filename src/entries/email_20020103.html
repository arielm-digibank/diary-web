Bangkok, 2002

Hello sirs,

We're back from the islands, landing at our home, Bangkok.

Life is back to normal, so we're back to writing

After 3 wild weeks, we're back to the routine (as much as possible in a trip).

We sommon-up things:

1 week in Ko-samoi: adjusting to the new environment: beaches outside our door, parties at night, coconut shake (or straight from the fruit itself). The island is very developed so we had some Burger-king also.

3 days in Ko-tao: better beaches, shnorkeling to nearer small islands, parties at night with people that do diving course.

1 week in ko-Pangan: wildest parties, everybody stoned from drinking, dancing until next noon, good music, cheap drinks (bucket of whiskey+redbool+kola = 15 shekel). A lot of fireworks.

After that we rested 2 days in Ko-samoi and we're back here.

In our plans: to go to straight to Laos.

We will leave on sunday evening.

Pretty soon we'll have our ticket to New-Zeland, so you'll get a better schedule.

Bye for now,

Sir      Sir

Gonen Malik
