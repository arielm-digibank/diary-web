In Bangkok again
Sat, 25 May 2002 22:21:40 -0700 (PDT)
Hi folks !!!!

We're back in Bangkok, some heavy travellers call it home.

Our stay in Philippines was magnificant.

From the last time....

We went to the big island of Palawan. It's very far from Manila, maybe 500 km. It's a 28 hours boat, it took us 43 hours to get there. Don't worry, you meet people and drink with them and the time passes with great pleasure and leasure.

In Palawan we went to a great coast - Sabang. A beach like we like - a hut 10 meter from the beach, hardly any tourists. There is also a natural park with the extrodinary underground river - 8.2 km long. We sailed only 1 kilometer inside and it was fabolous. Outside we met the local lizards - 2 meter long, heavy but harmless.

We couldn't stay more, we had to come back, but Palawan is the place to explore in the Philippines.

The ship delayed. This time 47 hours trip. We went to the rice terraces for only 2 hours, but we saw it, the famous one - mama teressa - one of 8 wonders of the world - a mountain that was curved to huge stairs of green rice fields - houndrads of meter high and kilometers long. All the place reminds us of north India - so it's a great place to travell to but we didn't have time.

So now we comlpete missing parts of Thailand: Kanchanaburi (the famous bridge on Quai river), Pataia (also famous). After that? Cambodia ? Hong-Kong ?

Don't forget we sympethize you all !! we really fond of you !!

Arik, the viking, Gonen
