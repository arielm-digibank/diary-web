special animals in nature
Fri, 22 Feb 2002 18:00:50 -0800 (PST)
Hello all !!!

In the past few days we saw a lot of animals in nature. Nature preservation is so important for the New-Zealand-ers. But sometime there are so many tourists that you feel like in museum.

First We went to Kaikura and saw some furry seals. Gonen got too close and the seal just roared at him. Gonen went back there for a picture - what a brave guy. It's funny, but when approaching them from behind they are agressive, but when approaching them from the front they are quite, you know from which side Gonen likes to come ....

It was a grey, a bit rainy day so we went to the hot springs of Hanmer, we sat there in the evening for 3 hours, doing what we know to do best - nothing. It was cold outside and warm in the pools, so it was great.

Day after that the sun came out just for the whale watching cruise of Malik . After 2 weeks they didn't sail bacause of the weather, Malik came and it was sunny and he saw 6-7 whales, albatroses and dolphins.

We drove from there to Omaru where we saw 2 kind of penguins - the yellow-eyed penguin. Very impressive and beautiful. We looked from above as they immerged from the sea and walked their funny walk to the beach at sunset. 2 hours after that we went to see the blue penguins, the smallest species. We watched them from close distance as they emerged from the sea. They also walk funny, but they are very small.

Now we came back to Dunedin for the weekend parties. Next week they'll start to study. We'll celebrate with them, but we'll leave the studying for them...

We still don't miss Israel, but we miss you guys !!!
