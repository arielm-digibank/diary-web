Places we saw in New-Zealand
Sat, 16 Mar 2002 23:10:35 -0800 (PST)
Hi all of our loyal readers !!!

It's been a while since our last Email. In the small towns we visited there is no Email or the Email is very expensive.

We are now in Nelson. The town is quite big ( population 50,000). The city is very nice. Rumors say that the night life here is great. After the small town we've passed, that turns to cemeteries after 17:00, the change should be refreshing.

Since the last Email we've been to Wanaka, a small and cute town where there is a nice trek (rob roy) and nice puzzling park with optic illusions.

We passed by queenstown and saw from a far all the expensive activities.

We went to the Glaciers (iceberg on the land), and it was amazing, a real ice in the middle of the land. We took a trek and walked on the ice. We lurked for each other slipping, of course the one that slips will be laughed upon for the rest of his life, so we both didn't slip.

We went along the west cost and saw some magnificant rocks emerging from the sea, few houndred meters from the beach. Very beautiful and wild. We saw some more seals.

Wev'e been to Mt. Cook, the highest mountain in New-Zealand and did some treks there.

We've passed through Arthur-pass, another extra-beatiful place. The road to and from there was also very beautiful.

We took a rest in maurina hot springs, our well-known hobby. It was great, pools outside and jakuzi inside. The only bad thing was the choise between sand-flies bitting us outside and seeing old and naked guys inside (the inside pools are seperated between man and women). In the inside pools, Malik insisted that we will stay with some cloth on. When Gonen went to refresh himself, and left Malik alone in the jakuzi, an old man, naked as at birth, some 80 years ago, entered the room and ofcourse, chose the small jakuzi to the big pool or showers. Seeing it made Gonen laugh from the door while Malik left the jakuzi upset.

Now we know that there is no ugly places in New-Zealand, we saw few modern factories and this is the only un-esthetic part we've saw. We understand that this could be the top beauty of nature. We understand it and accept it. When we'll back to reality to our modest coutry we'll wear sun-glasses or just look to the ground not to hurt our eyes until we'll get used to all the modern, high floors and ugly buildings.

We wish Hapoel Tel-Aviv good luck in Europe, more than it did in our country.

Canonball Gonen

Bullet Malik
