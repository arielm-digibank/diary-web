Hi to all the worried friends !!!

We are alright. This is the place with the cutest and kindest people (Yeah, break the NZ record of kindness). They treat us as kings here. As usual because of Malik the "eat as you can" deals doesn't pay of for them, but they are still nice.

We've been so far in a lot of places. We spend alot of time travelling: by bus and mainly by boats. We've seen a lot of places. The main places are:

The island of Bohol, south of Cebu. Gonen took a motorbike (beautiful Honda xlr) and we went to see the chocolate hills, an unexplained natural phenomena (We don't believe it once was underwater). The hills are round exactly like half a ball, more than 1200 of those hills, 20 to 100 meter high. On the way there a local family invited us in to their hut and we ate some local food, took pictures, talk and laughed. We stayed at the beach of Ponglao island, just near Bohol, great cost (and cheap cost too).

We also went to Borokay island, the most famous tourist island in the Philippines. White sand beaches and magnificant sunsets, the most beautiful island until now, it's just a pitty there are so many people there (mainly Philippino tourists).

Imagine getting a massage (20 shekel an hour) on the beach with great sunset in the far background you can see lightnings that light up the sky....

We feel good in the sailing boats, we didn't feel sick once.

Will you treat us the same when we'll comeback ?

Love and sympathy
